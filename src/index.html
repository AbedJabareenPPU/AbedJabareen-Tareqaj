The city of Hebron is located in Palestine and is considered the largest Palestinian governorate.

Why Hebron, specifically?

Hebron’s markets are distinguished by their traditional shops, which offer the best possible prices through ongoing, permanent promotional deals.

Distance: 30 km from the city center.
Duration: 2 hours to explore the markets.
Cost: $20 $15 per person.

Note: Prices may vary depending on the season and available offers.
