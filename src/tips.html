Travel Tips

Follow these simple tips to enjoy your trip to Hebron.

Before You Go

  • Plan the places you want to visit.
  • Check the weather before leaving.
  • Charge your phone.

During the Trip

  • Wear comfortable shoes.
  • Carry drinking water.
  • Respect local traditions and customs.
  • Keep the city clean.

Transportation

Plan how you will travel between places, and allow enough time for each visit.
